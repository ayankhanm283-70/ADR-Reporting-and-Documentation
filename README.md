# ADR Reporting & Documentation

## Objective
Complete a structured Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) reporting form based on a given patient case scenario.

## ADR Reporting Process

1. Collect patient information.
2. Document the suspected adverse reaction.
3. Record the suspected medication details.
4. Document the date and time of drug administration.
5. Record the onset and duration of the reaction.
6. Assess the seriousness of the ADR.
7. Document relevant medical history and concomitant medications.
8. Assess the outcome of the reaction.
9. Record dechallenge and rechallenge information when available.
10. Identify and document the suspected drug.
11. Submit the ADR report according to pharmacovigilance reporting requirements.

## Importance of ADR Documentation

Accurate ADR documentation helps in identifying potential drug-related safety issues, monitoring medicine safety, and supporting pharmacovigilance activities.

## Conclusion

A structured ADR report should contain accurate patient, drug, reaction, timeline, seriousness, outcome, and reporter information. Proper documentation supports effective pharmacovigilance and patient safety.
